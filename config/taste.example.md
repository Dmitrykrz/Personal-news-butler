## What a good digest looks like
- 3 to 7 items a day. Quality over quantity; a short digest is fine.
- Mostly NEWS (what is happening), about a quarter LEARNING (a tip or how-to I can try
  quickly).
- Each item: clear title, 2-3 sentence summary written from the actual article, link.
  No hype words, no filler.

## Likes

- Open source projects that solve a real problem.

## Dislikes
